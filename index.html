Twenty-Seven Churches by 2027

The mission of the Christian and Missionary Alliance, the denomination CCC belongs to, is to go and make disciples of all nations: to bring all of Jesus to all of the world.

As part of CCC’s Reach One More initiative, our goal is to catalyze twenty-seven church plants in their initial phases in least-reached places around the world by 2027.
